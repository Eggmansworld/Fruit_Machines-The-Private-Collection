# What Is This?

This collection of files is created for arcade game preservationists to faithfully preserve the game recreations of the Fruit Machine Emulation community. 

Up until early 2026, MFME was pretty much the sole remaining emulator that still had games being created for it, although that was dwindling as the interest in a code-locked emulator with no future pathway 100% created its own stagnance.

With the advances in AI and the proliferation of vibe coding, more than a fair share of new emulators have sprung to life. Most are targeting the newer video display cabinets, such as those made by companies like Novomatic/AGI/Astra/Bellfruit, Banilla, Barcrest, Blueprint, Electrocoin, Equinox, iBet, IGT, Inspired Gaming, MegaSlot, Prismatic, Scientific and Vantage. Unlike classic electromechanical, lamp-driven gambling cabinets, the advent of all-video cabinets drives costs down and increases interaction flexibility by dousing the player in a lot more eye candy. This might appeal to some players, but the old faithful will likely stick with the older lamped games for nostalgic purposes.

This collection exists to preserve the gaming creations of the FME community. Websites and forums don't last forever, so if and when they disappear all is not lost.

These recreations allow players and preservationists to experience machines that have largely disappeared from real-world venues.

# What Is Fruit Machine Emulation?

Fruit Machine emulation recreates classic UK fruit machines on a modern PC.

The games behave similarly to the original machines, including:

* spinning reels
* feature boards
* nudges and holds
* sound effects
* bonus features
* animated attract modes
* classic cabinet artwork

Many players enjoy Fruit Machine emulation for:

* nostalgia
* game preservation
* arcade history
* collecting
* gameplay discovery
* appreciation of classic machine design

# Why People Enjoy Fruit Machine Emulation

## Preservation

Many of these machines no longer exist in public venues. Emulation helps preserve an important part of gaming and arcade history.

## Nostalgia

For many players, these machines were a major part of UK pub and arcade culture.

## Variety

Fruit machines evolved dramatically over the years, from simple reel games to elaborate video-feature machines with animated boards and licensed themes.

## Discovery

New users are often surprised by how different UK fruit machines are compared to traditional slot machines.

# Popular Machine Styles You’ll Discover

## Classic Reel Machines

Traditional fruit machines with physical-style reels, nudges, holds, and gamble features.

## Club Machines

Larger, more advanced machines with:

* higher jackpots
* longer feature trails

* bigger bonus systems

## Video / Hybrid Machines

Later-era cabinets featuring:

* animated bonus boards
* large video displays
* digitized sound packages
* interactive feature systems
